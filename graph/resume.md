Confirmed preferences for Korean resumes and applications.

## Sentences

- When asked to split long sentences, preserve the information and divide the sentence structure; do not reduce content by default.
- Keep one main action or outcome per sentence. Do not pack purpose, input, processing, and result into one sentence.
- Avoid `및`; use `과/와`, commas, or separate sentences so the relationship is explicit.

## Content

- For a short field with a stated limit, use the space substantially when useful. For a 330-character project field, prefer 300 or more characters without padding or repetition.
- Add missing problem, role, implemented artifact, application path, or result instead of weak background.
- Replace vague phrases such as `흐름 구성` or `업무 흐름으로 연결` with the concrete action: executing a request, returning a result, showing it on screen, or connecting it through an API.
- Describe the person's work, choices, evidence, and effect instead of telling an evaluator what to value.

## Accuracy

- A formatting-only edit preserves confirmed claims, project names, technical scope, and duty wording.
- State a scope limit where it prevents overclaiming or changes how evidence should be read. Do not lead with defensive limitations that do not affect the claim.
