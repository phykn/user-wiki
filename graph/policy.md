What gets saved as durable guidance and where it goes.

## Confirmation

Save durable guidance only when the user:

- asks to save it, apply it, or use it from now on;
- confirms a rule you proposed; or
- says existing wiki content is stale or wrong.

A direct statement framed as a preference for future work across projects counts as confirmation. A correction for one task, casual wording, or your own inference does not: propose such a rule in chat without editing any durable file.

## Placement

- Preferences that apply across projects go in this wiki; rules for one project go in that project's instruction files.
- Leave out what agents do without being told, and what changes no behavior.
